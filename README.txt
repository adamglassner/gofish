GO FISH - A Lakeside Card Game  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
==================================================

Open Go Fish from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. The game opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge
(built into Windows 10 and 11).

HOW TO PLAY
- You play against Grandpa Walt, Captain Rosie and Little Finn. Everyone
  gets 5 cards and the rest go in the pond.
- On your turn, click a card to pick its rank, then click the person you
  want to ask. You can only ask for a rank you already hold.
- If they have any, they hand them all over and you go again. If not,
  it's "Go fish!" and you draw from the pond. Draw the rank you asked for
  and you fished your wish: go again.
- Four of a rank make a set, laid down on the dock automatically.
- When the pond runs out the game ends. Most sets wins.
- F2 = new game, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

Your record and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\GoFish, outside
the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by the game.
