# Changelog

All notable changes to Go Fish are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- First release of Go Fish for Windows.
- Play against three computer opponents on the dock: Grandpa Walt, Captain Rosie and Little Finn.
- Ask any opponent for a rank you hold. Catch their cards and go again, or Go Fish from the pond. Fish your wish and you go again too.
- Sets of four are laid down automatically and scored. The most sets when the pond runs dry wins.
- Every player's hand count, sets and whose turn it is are shown at all times, with a Fishing Log of every ask.
- Computer turns play out at a readable pace, with speech bubbles and the cards shown as they change hands. Choose Relaxed, Steady or Quick.
- Easygoing or Sharp opponents. Sharp ones remember who asked for what.
- Dawn-on-the-lake table with a wooden dock, reeds, lily pads and rippling water.
- HD fishing deck drawn in-house: old-angler kings, lady-angler queens with a fresh catch, dock-kid jacks with landing nets, fish aces and a leaping-trout card back.
- Three original calm acoustic tunes: "Morning on Loon Lake", "Dockside Drift" and "Cattail Lullaby", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects: a splash when you Go Fish, a reel-in when you catch the cards you asked for, and a chime when a set is laid down.
- Saved record (games, wins, sets, best streak) and settings, kept in %APPDATA%\Ironite\GoFish.
- Credits & Changelog screen and a How to Play guide.
