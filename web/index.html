How to Play Go Fish

You're on the dock with Grandpa Walt, Captain Rosie and Little Finn. Everyone gets 5 cards and the rest go in the pond.

  1. Ask. On your turn, click a card in your hand to choose its rank, then click the person you want to ask. You can only ask for a rank you already hold.
  2. Catch. If they have any cards of that rank, they must hand them all over, and you go again.
  3. Go fish. If they don't, they say "Go fish!" and you draw a card from the pond. If you happen to draw the rank you asked for, you fished your wish and go again. Otherwise the turn passes to the left.
  4. Sets. When anyone collects all four cards of a rank, the set is laid down on the dock automatically and scores 1.
  5. Running dry. If your hand runs out, you draw one card from the pond to keep going.
  6. The end. When the pond runs out, the game is over. Whoever has laid down the most sets wins.

Everyone at the table remembers who asked for what, so pay attention to the Fishing Log. Keys: F2 new game · M music on/off · F11 full screen
